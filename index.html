Founded in 2020 with a commitment to speed and accountability, DFASH Express helps Ibadan businesses and families move parcels reliably across Oyo State, Nigeria, and 120–230+ countries.

Dispatch Standards

Built on trust.
Driven by clarity.

Clear rate calculation, defined dispatch box dimensions, and strict safety rules ensure every accepted parcel arrives intact.

  1. 01

    Transparent Four-Factor Pricing

    Delivery quotes are calculated directly from distance (km), parcel weight, dimensions, and urgency — same-day express or scheduled pickup.

  2. 02

    Standard Dispatch Box Limit: 17″ × 17″ × 15″

    Standard dispatch bike boxes accommodate parcels up to 17″ × 17″ × 15″. Larger interstate and DHL commercial shipments are quoted separately.

  3. 03

    Prohibited & Unaccepted Items

    To prevent breakage and uphold air-cargo rules, we do not dispatch very fragile items (cakes, raw eggs, or glass drink bottles) or illegal/unauthorised goods.
