Rates & Box Size Limits

Delivery quotes are calculated fairly based on four transparent factors:

  • Distance (km): Exact route length across Ibadan, interstate corridors, or global zones.
  • Weight & Size: Actual and volumetric parcel dimensions.
  • Urgency: Same-day express dispatch or scheduled delivery window.
Standard Dispatch Box Limit 17″ × 17″ × 15″ Maximum dimensions for standard dispatch bike boxes. Larger commercial cargo is quoted separately.

Prohibited & Unaccepted Items

To keep every parcel intact and compliant with Nigerian and international air-cargo rules, DFASH Express does not dispatch:

  • Very Fragile Items: Cakes, raw eggs, and glass drink bottles.
  • Illegal or Unauthorised Goods: Contraband, restricted substances, or any item prohibited by Nigerian law or DHL international customs regulations.

Unsure whether your parcel qualifies for local or international shipping? Message our team on WhatsApp for instant confirmation before packing.
